How a page is made

Follow the ink.

Scroll slowly. Every job travels the same line from sketch to stack.

Step one

Sketch & set

We draw your layout, then compose it letter by letter from a cabinet of metal and wood type.

Step two

Mix & ink

Pigments are blended on a glass slab until the colour matches your swatch — and then a touch warmer.

Step three

Press

One sheet at a time, the platen closes with a soft thud. We pull proofs until the impression is just right.

Step four

Dry, trim, pack

Prints rest for a day, get trimmed against a steel rule, and travel in tissue to your door.
