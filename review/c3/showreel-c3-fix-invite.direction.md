# Direction card: showreel-c3-fix-invite
Fill before the build (design/direction.md: the card, and this channel's defaults to avoid). One line each:
"N. <Name>: <the choice, concrete, with times> | Why: <what it does for the viewer>". Not allowed as answers: default, steady, simple.

1. Hook: frame 0 is the party invite already lit on a fridge door, four fonts and six colours all centred, rung in red (NO HERO, 4 FONTS, 6 COLOURS) as the voice says "everything here is shouting" | Why: the viewer sees a mess they have made themselves in the first second, before any word explains it
2. Pacing curve: a change every 1.5-2 s; fastest at 0-1.2 s (three rings), a held pause of 3 s on the finished invite at 18-21 s | Why: the fixes land quickly and the calm invite gets time to be looked at
3. Camera: a push to 1.08 before each of the three fixes (the age, the font scan, the confetti), a pull back between them and for the before/after | Why: each fix is seen close, then the whole invite is judged
4. Light and mood: a warm lamp on a dark steel fridge door, amber marks every tool action (scan line, sparks), the invite goes from a busy pale blue to a calm cream as the mood score brightens | Why: amber means "the tool is working", so the invite itself carries the change
5. Type motion: one title line per fix (Fix 1 one hero) and a chip that pops on the word, the accent word in amber in the subtitles | Why: each fix has a name the eye can hold
6. Transitions: no cuts; the camera pushes and pulls and the invite changes in place; the before flashes back at the payoff | Why: one continuous object, so the change reads as cause and effect
7. Sound moments: opening sting 0 s, a stamp on "nothing stands out", a stack of lands as the old lines step down, a swell and three notes as the 7 grows, a scan roll as the fonts flip, a run of plucks as the confetti flies into the 7, the reveal chord on "read" | Why: every change is heard on its word
8. Rewatch moment: the font scan flipping every line, letter by letter, into one font at about 9-10 s | Why: it is the one move people scrub back to see happen
9. Payoff: the invite holds fully lit with the old shouting version as a tilted thumbnail, three ticks (7, name, date) and a green frame, then loops back to the shouting frame 0 | Why: the viewer keeps the before and the after side by side to copy
