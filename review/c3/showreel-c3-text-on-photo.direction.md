# Direction card: showreel-c3-text-on-photo
Fill before the build (design/direction.md: the card, and this channel's defaults to avoid). One line each:
"N. <Name>: <the choice, concrete, with times> | Why: <what it does for the viewer>". Not allowed as answers: default, steady, simple.

1. Hook: frame 0 is a gig poster on a night lamp post, white words lost in the glare of swinging spotlight beams, two red rings (WHITE ON GLARE, NOTHING BEHIND IT) and the claim "This poster is hard to read." over it; the beams sweep across the words in the first 2 s so they vanish as the eye reaches them | Why: anyone who has put white text on a photo sees their own poster in the first second
2. Pacing curve: a change every 1.5-2 s; fastest at 0-1.5 s (two rings, the beams swinging), three fixes at about 6, 8.8 and 11 s, a held 3 s on the finished poster with the before thumbnail at 17-21 s | Why: the fixes land quickly and the readable poster gets time to be looked at
3. Camera: a push to 1.08 before each of the three fixes (dim, bold, band) anchored on the poster's top edge, a pull back between them and for the before/after | Why: each fix is seen close, then the whole poster is judged
4. Light and mood: a night street, wall and wet pavement behind; the stage lights up as fixes land; amber marks every tool action (selection, scan line, sparks), green marks the finished poster | Why: amber means the tool is working, green means done, so colour tells the story
5. Type motion: one title line per fix (Fix 1 dim the photo, Fix 2 bold words, Fix 3 a solid band) and a chip that pops on the word; the poster's letters flip from regular to bold one by one on "bold"; accent words amber in the subtitles | Why: each fix has a name the eye can hold and the bold change is seen letter by letter
6. Transitions: no cuts; the camera pushes and pulls, the dark layer rises over the photo, the band slides out from the left edge, the before flashes back at the payoff | Why: one continuous object, so the change reads as cause and effect
7. Sound moments: sting at 0 s, ticks as the rings land, a drag as the cursor pulls the dark layer, a swap per letter line on "bold", a snap as the band lands, the reveal chord on "street", the paper flip at the proof | Why: every change is heard on its word
8. Rewatch moment: the three poster lines flipping to bold one after another at about 8.8-9.8 s on the dimmed photo | Why: it is the move people scrub back to see happen
9. Payoff: the poster holds dimmed, bold, with the date on a dark band, green frames on the title and the band, the crowd hopping, the BEFORE thumbnail tilted under the poster, then loops back to the glare of frame 0 | Why: the viewer keeps before and after side by side to copy
