"N. <Name>: <the choice, concrete, with times> | Why: <what it does for the viewer>". Not allowed as answers: default, steady, simple.

1. Hook: frame 0 is the salon price list already lit on a green wall, six rows centred so the prices wander, one red ring on the list (CENTRED ROWS) while the voice says "these prices are hard to compare" | Why: anyone who has centred a menu sees their own mistake in the first second
2. Pacing curve: a change every 1.5-2 s; fastest at 0-1.5 s (ring, zigzag across the prices), three fixes at about 6, 9 and 12 s, a held 3 s on the lined-up list at 17-20 s | Why: the fixes land quickly and the tidy list gets time to be looked at
3. Camera: a push to 1.07-1.09 before each of the three fixes (the right edge, the digits, the dots), a pull back between them and for the before/after | Why: each fix is seen close, then the whole list is judged
4. Light and mood: a warm room with a lit wall; amber marks every tool action (edge line, column guides, sparks), green marks the cheapest row and the finished frame | Why: amber means the tool is working, green means done, so colour tells the story
5. Type motion: one title line per fix (Fix 1 right edge, Fix 2 even digits, Fix 3 dot leaders) and a chip that pops on the word; accent words amber in the subtitles | Why: each fix has a name the eye can hold
6. Transitions: no cuts; the camera pushes and pulls and the prices glide to the edge in place; the before flashes back at the payoff | Why: one continuous object, so the change reads as cause and effect
7. Sound moments: sting at 0 s, ticks as the ring lands, a run of snaps as six prices meet the edge, a swap as the digits change width, plucks as the dots draw, the reveal chord on "cheapest", the paper flip at the proof | Why: every change is heard on its word
8. Rewatch moment: the six prices sliding together onto one right edge, one after another, at about 6-7 s | Why: it is the move people scrub back to see happen
9. Payoff: the list holds lined up, the cheapest row (Beard 100) in green with an eye line down the price column, the BEFORE thumbnail tilted under the list and a green frame, then loops back to the wandering prices of frame 0 | Why: the viewer keeps before and after side by side to copy
