1. Hook: frame 0 is the whole six-step map already built (line, loop, line back, every node named on a tag) with a chat reply typing word by word in a bubble above it: the whole sentence sits there dim and each word lights in turn with a cursor; the title asks WHY AI TYPES WORD BY WORD | Why: everyone has watched a chatbot type; the frame shows that exact thing and the system behind it at once, so the question is felt before it is read.
2. Pacing curve: hook 0-3.5 s, then six steps each starting on its spoken number (3.5, 7.0, 10.6, 14.0, 17.3, 21.4 s) with a 0.45-0.65 s move, then a 3 s hold where the step's panel builds part by part (a new element every 0.3-0.5 s); it speeds up in step 5 (17.3-21.4 s: the loop runner accelerates, one token per lap); the ONE held pause is the payoff, 25.6-29.6 s on the whole map | Why: the rhythm follows the voice (a move on every number), and the only long look is the picture the viewer keeps.
3. Camera: one continuous camera over one canvas, no cuts: push wide -> step 1 at 3.5 s (0.6 s), right to step 2 at 7.0 s, down the line to the model at 10.6 s, small shifts around the loop at 14.0 and 17.3 s (0.45 s), across to the stream at 21.4 s (0.65 s), pull back to the whole map at 24.9 s (0.75 s); each hold drifts 10-12 world px a second with a slight push; every flight starts from the exact hold position (no snap) | Why: the camera follows the action (out, round the loop, back) and every move has a reason: the next step.
4. Light and mood: dark graphite ground tinted 11 % with the step's colour; each node has its own hue (1 blue, 2 amber, 3 violet, 4 pink, 5 green, 6 coral) and casts a soft torch-like wedge of that hue outward, the lit node's wedge strongest, the rest 25-30 %; dim sub-nodes, a faint dot grid and slow dust fill every empty zone; no blur stacks | Why: one subject lit per beat (focus) while the frame is never empty, and the text keeps its contrast at phone size.
5. Type motion: a fixed two-line title (38 px css, 76 px at 1080) and a small logo chip that never move; the outlined step pill rolls to "0N / 06 · NAME" mid-flight; word-by-word captions with the key word in the step colour and a colon pause after every number ("One: your question"); one big kinetic moment: SHORTER = FASTER wipes in on a tilted banner across the top third at 25.2 s and holds | Why: a frame the eye can trust (structure) and one big type hit on the takeaway.
6. Transitions: no cuts; 0.5 s before each move the next card shows as a dashed ghost in its place and its link draws once (the one short line draw, cause -> effect); the camera then follows it; panels fade and scale in (never pop) as the camera leaves and lands | Why: anticipation from the reference, and the viewer sees where we go before we go.
7. Sound moments: a swell under the opening reply with soft ticks as its words light (0.3-3 s); a swish on each move and a snap on each landing (3.5, 7.0, 10.6, 14.0, 17.3, 21.4 s); blips as panel parts land; a tick per token added in the loop (about 18-25 s, quickening); a thud and a resolve chord as SHORTER = FASTER lands (25.3 s); a bed under the voice | Why: the ear catches the loop speeding up and the takeaway landing.
8. Rewatch moment: 17.3-21.4 s, the loop closes and a bright green runner goes round it faster and faster; each time it passes ADD IT ON a new token pops into the answer row (Sun, light, scatters, off, air) | Why: the viewer sees the reply being built one lap at a time, the surprise of the reel, worth scrubbing back.
9. Payoff: 24.9-29.6 s: the pull-back to the whole map (the same framing as frame 0), the loop still running slower, SHORTER = FASTER on the banner and the caption "So a shorter answer means fewer loops, and it arrives faster" held to the last frame | Why: one useful rule the viewer can act on, and the last frame matches the first so the replay loops.
