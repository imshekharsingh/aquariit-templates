# Direction card: showreel-pv-tech-2d5-test (preview for comparison, never posted)

1. Hook: frame 0 shows the hub already alive (a phone with contactless ripples under a spotlight cone) while the map's other five cards pop in around it and the lines draw outward; the question "what happens after you tap" is the fixed two-line title | Why: the viewer sees a system being built, and a thing already moving, not a label.
2. Pacing curve: opening push 0-2.2 s, one hub hold 2.2-3.3 s (the ONE pause, the voice says "here is what happens next"), then six flights of 1.2 s at 3.3, 7.9, 12.0, 16.0, 19.8, 23.7 s, each followed by a 2.5-3 s hold where the step's interface builds part by part (a new element every 0.4-0.7 s); pull-back 27.4-29.4 s | Why: a new visual idea about every second inside a steady one-reason-per-move rhythm.
3. Camera: one continuous camera over one canvas, no hard cut: push (0-2.2 s), six fly moves hub -> step 1 ... step 6 with the scale dipping mid-flight, slow drift under every hold (3.5% push), final pull-back to the whole map | Why: every move goes to the next step of the story.
4. Light and mood: graphite ink, one colour world per step (hub warm cream, token cyan, terminal lime, acquirer orchid, network coral, bank gold, approved mint), a spotlight cone and a radial glow behind the active card, everything else dims to 20 % but stays in place | Why: one subject lit at a time; the colour change tells the viewer a new step began.
5. Type motion: the fixed title at 34 px (accent on TAP TO PAY, takes the step colour), the section pill rolls to "0N / 06 · NAME" on each flight, card names at 23 px, the stamp APPROVED slams 1.6x -> 1 at 25.6 s; word-by-word captions pop on their word with the key word in the step colour | Why: the title and pill never move (a frame the eye can trust); the stamp is the one big type moment.
6. Transitions: no cuts; each flight is led by a bright light pulse that travels the line from card to card and the camera follows it; the line into each step lights as it arrives | Why: the cause of every move is visible on screen.
7. Sound moments: a swell into each flight with a snap on arrival (3.3-24.9 s), blips as each interface part lands, ticks on the checklists (13-23 s), a low thud on the APPROVED stamp at 25.65 s, a bed under the voice | Why: sound supports the motion; the stamp is the loudest moment.
8. Rewatch moment: 25.6 s, the APPROVED stamp slams, a ring bursts, and a mint pulse runs back along every line to the hub (the answer returns the same way) | Why: the payoff of the whole map is seen at once.
9. Payoff: 27.4-30 s, the camera pulls back to the whole lit map with the reply pulse still running; the last frame is the full six-step trip, which loops back to the hub-first opening | Why: the viewer keeps the whole system as one picture.
