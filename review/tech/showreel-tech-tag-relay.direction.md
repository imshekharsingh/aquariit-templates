## Motion-design pass (entrance, cascade, build, life after landing)
- The set exists at frame 0 behind the window; what builds is each link (drawn once, 0.4 s, just before its move) and each card's content.
- Card 1: the pill sits where the window's pill was; the tag is drawn with its pulse rings (radius and opacity are sines of t), the label rises from the right.
- Card 2: the toggle slides in, its knob moves and the track turns amber (0.35 s), the label rises, the chip pops, three wave arcs flicker next to the phone.
- Card 3: the padlock pops in, the "WHERE" chip slides right into it and shrinks away, the shackle drops shut, the line rises.
- Card 4: the rack's rows are there, their LEDs breathe; a small padlock slides in from the left and disappears into the rack; the eye and "CANNOT READ IT" arrive from the sides, the slash fades in.
- Card 5: the map plate rises, four trail dots appear one by one, the pin drops with a back ease, two rings pulse round it, the label rises.
- Card 6: 22 dots appear on 0.035 s steps and breathe, thin links draw in, "900 MILLION+" rises, then "GALAXY DEVICES".
- Life after landing: node bob none, sub-nodes bob 4 px slowly, wedges breathe 6 %, dust drifts, the camera drifts in every hold.
- Glitch rules (owner note 2): every value is a continuous function of t; no modulo wraps, no per-frame random, no instant text swaps (letters and captions fade), no will-change layers; Output QA FLICKER on a 60 fps local capture before the render.
