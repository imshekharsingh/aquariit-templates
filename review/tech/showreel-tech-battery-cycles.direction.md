# Direction card: showreel-tech-battery-cycles
Fill before the build (design/direction.md: the card, and this channel's defaults to avoid). One line each:
"N. <Name>: <the choice, concrete, with times> | Why: <what it does for the viewer>". Not allowed as answers: default, steady, simple.

1. Hook: frame 0 is a glowing pouch battery cell, opened like an x-ray, with a tally of marks already being scratched into its glass by amber sparks; "500" slams on the spoken word at 2.2 s and swaps to cyan "1000" on "double" at 4.0 s | Why: a rating shown as scratches on the thing itself says "this has a limited number of lives" before any word, and the number doubling is the surprise.
2. Pacing curve: a new view every 2-4 s: front 0-3.6 s, low macro 3.6-5.0 s, ring 5.0-9.7 s with an orbit, push to the cell 9.7-10.9 s, speeds up through the 80% / 500 / 1000 shots 11-22 s; the ONE held pause is the side-by-side frame of both cells at 17.5-19.2 s, held 1.7 s | Why: the hold lets the viewer compare 500 against 1000 marks and gives a frame worth saving.
3. Camera: A front push then low macro looking up at the second block (0-5 s); B wide orbit then push into the cell (5-11 s); C low three-quarter in a cyan edge-lit room, pull to front with the gauge, slide across the marks (11-17 s); D close on the 500 cell, pull back as the 1000 cell slides in, push on its marks, back out (17-22 s); E orbit round the cell in front of the target rings (22-25 s); F a receding row of four worn cells, a closer look at 27 s (25-29 s); G settle back to A's framing (29-34 s) | Why: one named move per shot keeps the object alive without ever sitting on one picture.
4. Light and mood: dark ink room, one lit hero (cool rim, warm kicker), half the frame near-black; teal glass for the charge, warm white for the marks, amber only for the 80% line, the target rings and the hot cell, cyan for the better number (1000, CHECK); no lit floor | Why: the cell and its marks glow while the rest recedes, matching the gold look and avoiding a flat grey wash.
5. Type motion: one bold word or number at a time, 150-270 px in the empty top band: 500 and 1000 swap with a small pop on the spoken word, CYCLE tracks in then 100%, iPHONE 14 then 80% then 500, iPHONE 15 then 1000, TARGET stamps then DEADLINE drops and is struck through in amber, HABITS wipes in, CHECK rises in cyan | Why: the number is the story and it changes only when the voice says it.
6. Transitions: push into the cell with a cyan flash (A to B), rack-focus dissolve (B to C), light wipe (C to D), whip pan (D to E), rack focus (E to F), push with a warm flash (F to G); each is caused by the next idea starting | Why: four different kinds so no two neighbouring cuts feel alike.
7. Sound moments: impact on "500" 2.2 s, impact and zaps on "double" 4.0 s, four rising ticks as the ring quarters close 7.1-8.6 s, impact on "100" 9.1 s then a snap and thud as the single mark scratches in 10.1 s, impact on "80", snap on "deadline", a riser that crests on the last word with a chime | Why: the ear catches each number and the ring closing.
8. Rewatch moment: 4.0-5.0 s, the second block of 500 marks scratches in row by row in under a second while the camera drops to a low macro, doubling the tally | Why: viewers scrub back to count that the marks really did double.
9. Payoff: last 2 s: CHECK in cyan over the cell with a "Battery health" card, then the tally wipes clean so the cell loops straight back into frame 0, where scratching starts again | Why: the viewer keeps one action (check your battery health) and the loop restarts the count.
