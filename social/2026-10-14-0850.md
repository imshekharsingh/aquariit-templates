# 2026-10-14-0850 · Suddly (clay-soft laundry pickup subscription for a local laundry business)

## Hook
Why do busy customers skip your laundry?

## Caption
Laundry shop website idea: customers book a pickup in one tap, you collect tonight and deliver folded in 48 hours, billed weekly.
Soft clay design with wobbly soap bubbles, a Solo / Pair / Family plan grid and a 3-step "Book, Bag, Back" flow. Original concept, fictional brand "Suddly".
Send this to a laundry or dry-cleaning owner who closes at 7, and follow for a new template every day.
