# Audience: people's choice

Owner decision (13 Oct 2026): the India-first market rules are retired. There is no fixed country, currency or list of business types.

## What is sold never changes
Aquarii Tech Studio's own services (design/services.md): web design and development, social media management, website + security management, CDN/security
aggregation. "People's choice" decides the market, the currency and which buyer pain to lead with, never what we sell. The template's business type is only proof.

## How to choose, every template
1. Read the latest "Instagram collect" workflow log: which reels got the most reach, watch time, shares, saves and comments, and which business types they showed.
2. Read the comments and DMs the owner shares: what do people ask for ("do one for a gym", "how much for this")? A request is the strongest signal.
3. Use audience countries when the insights show them; pick the currency and price style that audience uses. With no data yet, choose freely and vary it.
4. Keep rotating service lines (and the template's business types) so the numbers can compare them; note in design/experiments.md which choice was a test.

## The one rule that stays
Never present invented results as Aquarii's own work or clients. Numbers inside a fictional brand's page are example content: fine in the page, never the hook,
never "we got X for a client".
