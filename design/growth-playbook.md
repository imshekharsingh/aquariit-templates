# Growth playbook for @aquarii.webstudio (the advisor's standing plan)

Owner of the plan: the advisor (Claude). Owner of the account and the daily tap on "Post": the account owner. Budget: zero. Everything here is free.
Update this file whenever an experiment (design/experiments.md) settles something; old advice that the numbers disproved gets deleted, not kept.

## Goal and audience
The account is a shop window for Aquarii Technologies (web design and build). Success = enquiries from business owners and founders, with followers as the means.
The market is India and the studio is brand new: see design/market.md (₹ prices a beginner can really quote, no invented results, concepts for Indian small businesses).
So each reel is written for the **buyer**: a small-business owner or founder asking a money / time / results question ("What should a website cost?",
"Where is your website losing money?"). Designers and developers are a bonus audience, not the target.

## What the evidence says (check before believing; sources are third-party marketing blogs, not Instagram itself)
- Reach to strangers is driven mainly by sends (DM shares) and by how much of the reel is watched; likes matter little. A new account's reels are first shown to a small
  test group; they expand only if that group watches and sends.
- Our first five reels (format A: static title card, 24-30 s) got average watch of 2.5-4.6 s, reach ~20-100 each and no shares: the first seconds lose people.
- Original content is favoured, reposts are penalised. Trial Reels (test on non-followers) need about 1,000 followers. Hashtags: at most 5 per post.

## Content rules (also in CLAUDE.md for the daily generator)
1. Moves from frame 0. No static title card, no logo intro.
2. Hook on screen in the first 3 s: a question of at most 8 words that the ending answers. Never promise what the video does not show.
3. About 18 s. Payoff (the answer) in the last 4 s. Cut anything that does not serve the hook.
4. Caption: first line = what it is, in the words a buyer would search; last line asks for a send ("Send this to a founder who..."); then one keyword line (the pasted text is caption + keywords).
5. Hashtags are optional now (at most 1-2); keywords in the caption do the searching. Cover = the cover card image, set in Instagram, not part of the video.
6. One idea per reel, one next step (the CTA the template ends on).

## Operating rhythm
Daily (owner, about 10 min): post the reel from the "Reel ready" issue at a consistent time, tick "Reel uploaded", reply to every comment in the first hour, leave 3-5
genuine comments on posts by your audience (small-business owners, founders, agencies). No automation, no mass actions.
Daily (automatic): the template and its reel are made and rendered; the Instagram collector reads the numbers overnight.
Weekly (advisor): read the per-reel table, update design/experiments.md, open a verdict issue, set the next experiment. The next template concepts follow what worked.

## Decision thresholds (format B, 18 s; adjust as the account grows)
- Average watch time at least 6-8 s (about 40% of the reel) = the hook and pace work. Under 4 s = rework the first 3 seconds.
- Shares + saves at least 1% of reach = the idea resonates. Zero after 5+ reels = change the angle, not the format.
- Reach per reel rising across reels = the algorithm is expanding us; keep the format and vary only one thing.
Small samples lie: decide on 3+ reels per variant, never one.

## Profile checklist (owner does it once; the advisor drafts the text)
- Account type Creator (check the audio library), clear name + keyword in the name field ("Aquarii | web design studio").
- Bio: who it is for + the result + the action; link-in-bio goes to a public page with a contact method (to be built: needs the owner's email / WhatsApp / booking link).
- Profile photo and 3 pinned reels: the best-performing ones, once data exists.

## Never
Buying followers, engagement pods or comment-swap groups, follow/unfollow loops, mass or automated DMs and comments, reposting other people's content, scraping,
logging bots, banned or stuffed hashtags, claims the reel does not show. They risk the account and the numbers they produce are worthless.

## Stages
0-100 followers: consistency, hook testing, replies, buyer-focused ideas. 100-1,000: repeat what works as a recognisable series, collaborate with small accounts in the niche.
1,000+: Trial Reels for cold testing, case-study reels from real client work, a lead magnet behind the link.
