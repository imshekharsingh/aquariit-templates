## 1. What actually happened (19 days, ~25 posted reels)
- **10 followers** from ~15,000 total reach (0.07%). 99% of reach is strangers. 0-1 saves, 0-8 shares, ~0 comments per reel.
- **Reach has two flat levels and nothing ever grows:** 8 reels at ~1,050-1,280 (Instagram's first test pool), the rest at 105-170. Every
  pool reel was a world icon in a state nobody pictured; the gate for that works (8 of 8). No reel ever got past the first pool.
- **Watch was not the wall:** eiffel-red held 63% of 23 s and still stopped at 1,145. So the pool is not closed by length alone; it is
  closed because the ~1,100 strangers who see a reel do nothing with it (no send, no save, no comment, no follow).
- **We changed the machine, not the offer:** 7 formats in 19 days (2D, 3D, 2.5D flow, journey, guess, story engine, Echoes). Each change
  cost days of engine work, ~$4+ a reel and owner complaints about the look; no format ran long enough to read, and the grid looks like
  7 different channels, so a stranger who taps the profile sees no reason to follow.

**The real cause:** a Turns Out reel is a one-off fun fact about a monument. It gives a stranger nothing to keep (save), nothing to say
(comment), nobody to send it to (share) and no promise of more (follow). Our web account grows because each reel is something the viewer
*has* (a bug on their own phone: 54 saves on one reel). Turns Out needs the same: a reel about something **the viewer believes**.

## 2. The bet: "You were taught this wrong"
Keep what works (a famous thing everyone pictures one way, in frame 0) and change what the viewer feels: from "huh, a fact" to
"wait, *I* believed that". A myth the viewer holds makes them comment ("I believed this!"), send it ("show this to my dad") and follow
for the next one. "Turns out..." is already the myth-buster's phrase; the name fits better than it ever did.

**Topic gate (all 4 yes, on paper, before any build):**
1. Most people on every continent believe it or picture it this way (a school-book myth, a famous "fact", an icon's wrong name).
2. The truth is checked in 2 primary or reliable sources (museum, official body, space agency, encyclopedia), and no expert body disputes it.
3. The myth and the truth can be DRAWN as simple objects or places (a helmet, a ship, a building, a map, a timeline); no photo, no face.
4. Not on the never-list (channel3.md): no health or diet myths, religion, nations ranked, living or recent people, tragedies, India 16a.

## 3. One frozen format for 4 weeks (no engine or kit work on Turns Out until 18 Dec)
- **Engine (21 Nov: the UI look, design/channel3.md "UI look", replaces the sketch plates):** the Echoes engine with code-drawn `sketch` plates (owner, 20 Nov: no media downloads, "highly time consuming and waste of
  resource"; design/channel3.md "Sketch plates"). Per reel we write only the config and, when a myth needs a new object, one drawing in SK. Any engine bug found is noted in design/todo.md and fixed after the 4 weeks, unless it
  breaks a reel (then the smallest fix).
- **Length 15-22 s, 40-55 words, 6-8 photos, a new element every ~2 s** (rule 11 holds).
- **0.0 s:** the myth as everyone pictures it, full-bleed, with the cover title in the centre 3:4 (`COVER` check), e.g. "VIKINGS NEVER
  WORE THESE". Voice from 0.0 s, the myth denied in 8 words or fewer. No question, no countdown, no logo.
- **1-13 s:** where the myth came from (the satisfying part: the opera costume, the cartoonist, the mistranslation), then the truth,
  3 beats, each one photo and one fact landing on its spoken word.
- **13-20 s:** "Turns out, ..." payoff, then loop to frame 0. No call to action in the video (owner, 21 Oct: reels end on the reel itself);
  the send line lives in the caption: "Send this to whoever told you ..."
- **Caption:** line 1 = the myth as people search it ("Did Vikings wear horned helmets? No."), line 2 = "Which myth did you believe
  longest? Comment it, we will check it next." (the comment ask feeds the topic list), sources line, sources pinned as the first comment.
- **Series tag:** none on screen (E22 never read). The bio carries the promise instead (section 5).
