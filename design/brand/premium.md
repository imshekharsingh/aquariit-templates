# Premium film style: "cinematic tech" (owner, 15 Oct 2026)
Goal: every Aquarii reel looks like a premium tech brand's product film. Restraint is the look.

- **One idea per frame.** 2-4 words of display type, never captions + labels + HUD at once. Empty space is part of the design.
- **Three colours.** Navy ink (#070B16) and soft blue light; white type; ONE lime accent for the answer. Coral only for the single "problem" word.
- **Material and light.** Glass nodes, thin light beams, soft studio spotlights, depth (focus on the subject, the rest falls into dark). Real 3D (P.three) for the hero shot.
- **Calm motion.** Long-tail easing (P.EASE.glide), no bounce, no shake, no flashes. Camera moves take 1 s or more. After every reveal, hold 1-1.5 s.
- **Length 26-30 s.** About 7 beats; each beat says one thing and lets it land.
- **Voice.** af_heart at 0.95 speed (max 1.05), about 2 words/s, a pause after each sentence; "we", warm, confident, no hype.
- **Sound.** Sparse and tactile: soft clicks on reveals, one low swell into the hero, a gentle chime on the answer (licensed CC0 pack). No arcade sounds.
- **Ending.** The brand card: a pain-point question, "DM us to know more", the keyword line, the logo.
- **Never.** Neon arcade HUDs, particle explosions, screen shake, many colours, crowded frames, rushed speech.
Parts: P.sys + its premium helpers (glassNode, beam, pulse, spot), P.three for the hero shot, P.brandEnd.

## Full, not empty (owner, 15 Oct 2026, after v4)
Premium is rich detail kept in order, not an empty screen. Every frame has three layers: the subject (one focal point), supporting detail (labels, counters,
small UI, people, pulses) and a depth layer (dotted globe, soft light, grid). The voice is near-continuous at a natural pace (about 2.7 words/s); the only
silence is the short breath before the reveal. Impacts are clean light (a ring flare), never debris.
