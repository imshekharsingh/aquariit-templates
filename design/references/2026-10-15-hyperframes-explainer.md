# HyperFrames explainer doctrine (studied 15 Oct 2026; Apache-2.0 skill docs, principles only, no code copied)
Source: the faceless-explainer "motion-language" and "cut-catalog" references of the open HyperFrames skills. What we take:
- **One continuous film, one camera.** A mechanism explainer is a world the camera travels through, not slides that animate once and freeze.
- **Reveal on the voice.** Each component appears when the voice names it, mostly in the later half of its scene; nothing dumped in the first quarter.
- **Smooth long-tail easing by default** (our P.EASE.glide / P.SPRING.default); overshoot only for playful game moments (a power-up landing).
- **No lazy breathing, no aimless drift.** Aliveness comes from the subject doing its job (packets flowing, counters ticking, a shield taking hits).
- **Velocity-matched seams.** Zoom-through (forward = progressing, inverse = arriving) and cut-the-curve (both sides move the same way at the same speed), with blur on the fast part.
- **Deterministic.** No random or wall-clock motion; variation comes from indices and seeded noise.
Used first in film/2026-10-15-0850 v3 (P.sys toolkit in scripts/film/parts.js).
