## B. What hooks people in short-form tech / explainer reels (craft)

What the platform says it ranks (primary-ish): for Reels, **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach**; sends weigh most for
reaching non-followers (Adam Mosseri, Jan 2025, reported by [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/),
[Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389/),
[Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/blog/creator-news/whats-working-on-instagram-right-now-carousels-and-sends-says-adam-mosseri)). So a reel must
(1) stop the thumb, (2) be watched to the end and replayed, (3) be worth sending. Our own data agrees: the two richest drawn worlds
(night-mode 1,468 views, screen-120hz 958) beat the median 123 by 8-12x (design/rules-tech.md).

Most "hook science" online is vendor blogs; the numbers in them are unverified, the techniques are consistent. Ten techniques we will use:

1. **The picture is the hook, at frame 0, not a title.** Viewers decide in ~2-3 s; open on the most surprising frame of the reel (the inside
   of the gadget, the thing mid-change), claim in 3-5 s. ([CapCut hook patterns](https://www.capcut.com/create/short-form-video-hooks-first-3-second-patterns),
   [dev.to hook engineering](https://dev.to/bulkdl/hook-engineering-the-science-of-the-first-3-seconds-in-short-form-video-2026-2jp9);
   our own night-mode had the answer split in frame 0.)
2. **Motion already happening at 0 s.** Start mid-action (chips already lifting, light already flowing); a still first frame reads as an ad
   or a slideshow. ([Influencers Time on loops](https://www.influencers-time.com/loop-optimized-reels-the-seamless-cut-that-boosts-rewatch-ra/))
3. **One visual metaphor carries the whole idea** (Branch Education's narrated, fully animated teardowns lean on analogy: "a camera is like an
   eye"). Ours: memory chips *leave* the phone and feed the AI server. ([Branch Education, Bluetooth notes](https://thereflex.nl/posts/branch-education-how-does-bluetooth-work),
   [BlenderNation on their SoC film](https://www.blendernation.com/2020/12/04/25min-video-exploring-how-smartphone-processors-work/))
4. **The camera travels into the object.** The agency tech-explainer staple is the dive: macro on the part, push through the shell, pull out
   to reveal scale. ([XO3D, smartphone ads](https://xo3d.co.uk/blog/social-media-adverts-for-smartphones/), [Behance iPhone motion piece](https://www.behance.net/gallery/171243759/iPhone-14-Pro-3D-Animation))
5. **Scale jumps between shots** (macro, then wide, then macro): each cut changes size by 3x or more, so every 1.5-2.5 s the eye gets a new
   picture without visual clutter. (Same sources; our own critic note: heart-green held one side view for 36 s.)
6. **One bold phrase on screen at a time, captions kept stable and separate.** Moving every word kills hierarchy; one primary phrase, the
   rest smaller or later. ([CapCut kinetic type](https://www.capcut.com/features/how-to-make-kinetic-typography-capcut-workflow),
   [Advids, cognitive load](https://advids.co/insights/the-cognitive-load-paradox-why-engaging-animation-reduces-comprehension-and-how-to-fix-it))
7. **The number is the hero when there is a number.** A huge numeral changing (8 GB to 4 GB, 700%) is understood muted in under a second
   (much viewing starts muted; the claim of 68% is unsourced, the advice to design for mute is universal).
8. **A clean premium product look**: dark clean ground, one light source, reflections and materials on the object, nothing else in frame.
   ([Alici case study on product reveals](https://alici.ai/formulas/media/unreal.mechanics/f5b7210c-6172-5457-bb59-9b0c92d80ecb-mechanical-product-reveal-ai-video))
9. **The loop is designed backwards**: the last frame flows into the first (same framing, mid-motion, sound beat on the seam), so the
   replay counts as watch time. ([CapCut seamless loops](https://www.capcut.com/create/seamless-loop-videos-continuity-techniques),
   [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/double-youtube-watch-time-looping-shorts))
10. **End on a send reason, not a "save this" card**: a useful verdict ("buy 8 GB now if you keep phones 4 years") plus a one-word
    comment question ("How much RAM is in yours?"). Sends are the top signal for non-followers (Mosseri, above).

Sound (from the same guides and our SOUND rules): one hit on every cut, a riser into the reveal, the strongest beat on the loop seam.
