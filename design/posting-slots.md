# Posting slots (suggestion from real data, 24 Nov 2026)

## Can Instagram tell us when the audience is online?
**No.** The collector asks for `online_followers` on every channel; the API accepts the call (SUPPORTED) but returns **no rows** for all three accounts
(web 484 followers, tech 13, Turns Out 9), so there are no audience-online hours. Follower and reach demographics work for web (countries, cities, age, gender) and are empty for tech and Turns Out.
Slots below therefore come from post time vs reach: **low confidence**, because topic moves reach far more than hour does.

## Evidence
- Reach is 97-99% non-followers on all channels (web 9,920 of 10,207 in 7 days), so followers being awake matters little; the audience is spread over India, Germany, USA, Indonesia and UK.
- Web, 40 posts: median reach by IST hour of posting: 12-13h 1,311 (11 posts), 14-17h 1,883 (4), 18-21h 1,436 (5), before 12h 1,248 at 10h (4). Small groups, mixed topics.
- Web, same day, two posts: the later one beat the earlier one on 4 of 5 days (7 Nov 19:32 4,157 vs 13:53 2,772; 5 Nov 18:58 1,485 vs 12:44 1,260; 16 Nov 22:21 2,196 vs 12:08 1,372; 25 Oct 17:17 1,414 vs 11:37 275), lost on 6 Nov (20:15 1,196 vs 13:02 1,311). The earlier post's topic confounds this.
- Tech, 28 posts: hour shows nothing clean. Settled reels at 1,000+ went out at 12:22, 12:53, 14:37, 22:09, 22:24 and 23:07; the misses (129, 148, 347) at 12:50, 10:21 and 14:49. Topic and the 15 Nov change explain it, not the hour.
- Turns Out, 7 reels: best 1,115 (08:29) and 749 (18:24); 22-23h posts 124-260. Too few to say.
- The 30-day audit reads web 13:00 best (median 3,768, 4 reels, carried by the 107k reel), so it is not independent evidence.

## Suggested slots (inside 13:00-21:00 IST, 2 h apart; all low confidence)
| Channel | Slot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tech | 15:00 | 14-15h tech posts: 1,483 (14:37), 347 (14:49); midday and late evening show no gap, so it is the open slot that keeps 2 h from the others |
| Turns Out | 17:00 | 18:24 post reached 749 vs 124-260 at 22-23h; 17:00 stays clear of web |
| Web | 19:00 | later same-day post won 4 of 5 pairs; 18-21h median 1,436 vs 12-13h 1,311 (5 vs 11 posts) |

Not a reason to move yet: same-day pairs and the hour table can each be one topic effect. A clean test: web at 19:00 for 5 days against the 12-13h median of 1,311, nothing else changed.
