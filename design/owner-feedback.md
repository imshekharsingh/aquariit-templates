# Owner feedback checklist (read before calling ANY reel ready)

Every line is something the owner had to catch himself. Before saying "ready", check the FINISHED output (the rendered MP4, its audio,
its frames on a phone-sized screen) against every line, as a viewer would, not the scene code or the cue list. Add a line each time the
owner catches something new, the same day.
Each line also becomes an automated Output QA check the same day (scripts/qa/output-qa.py, run on GitHub on the final MP4 after every render;
owner, 12 Nov 2026): a reel whose Output QA fails is NOT READY. A line no script can measure says so and stays a critic question.

- 5 Nov: a tech term said wrong ("Wi-Fi" read as two words). Listen for every technical word.
- 7 Nov: a reel barely moved (one small phone still for 18 s). Does something change every 1.5-2 s?
- 7 Nov: a developer-only topic. Would a non-developer feel it within 1 s?
- 7 Nov: text over text, labels over UI, subtitles slicing the subject, hook over the device, action under Instagram's right-side buttons.
- 7 Nov: motion jumps and dead stretches; "no premium feel" (flat mock-up, no light or depth).
- 7 Nov: a reel had to be reported as posted. Read the post check, never ask.
- 7 Nov: web stuck under 2k reach for 9 reels and nobody flagged it. Read the trend lines every day.
- 8 Nov: too much packed into 20 s, rushed. Does each motion have time to land?
- 8 Nov: Turns Out "flat": one bridge from start to end. Count the scenes and the transitions.
- 8 Nov: tick sounds missing. They existed in the cue list at 5% volume and were inaudible. LISTEN to the finished audio: can you hear
  a sound for every element that moves, small high, medium mid, large low?
- 8 Nov: the whooshes and swells were too much. Do the element details (ticks, blips, snaps) lead, with the air soft and behind them?
- 8 Nov: a figure's action looked like a fall; human actions need anticipation, ease and weight; check every 0.1 s.
- 8 Nov: Turns Out "feels flat" even with a journey. Does every world have 4+ depth planes with air between them, something passing close to the lens, contact shadows and one light (qa.mjs DEPTH lines)? Look at the frames: do far things look far?
- 9 Nov: the napping prince "looks like someone has abruptly fallen". Is every figure action built from keyframed joints (Kt.act) and read on a 0.1 s strip of the frames, with ambient life (dust, cloth, lamp light) and its quiet sound in every world?
- 8 Nov: tech info felt cut off ("I feel lost at times"). Does every line say what, why and what it means for you, joined by a link word?
- 8 Nov: "we lean too much on Apple." Would an Android owner feel the reel is for them too?
- 8 Nov: tech made clearer by slowing the voice to 29 s; the owner wanted clearer sentences at a natural pace, 20-22 s. Is the reel 20-22 s at about 3 words/s (voice check), with clarity from the sentences, not the speed?

The principle (owner, 8 Nov 2026: "Who is going to have that critical thinking?"): a check that measures the input (code, cue lists,
counts) is not a check. Measure what the viewer gets: the rendered video and its sound.
