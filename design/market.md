# Market profile (read before every template and every hook)

**Market:** India first. **Seller:** a brand-new web studio (Aquarii), one to a few people, no case-study portfolio yet. Content must speak from that real position:
what the studio can honestly quote, build and deliver today. Update this file whenever the owner gives real numbers; everything marked PLACEHOLDER is a proposal,
not a fact, until the owner confirms it.

## Money
- Always ₹ with Indian digit grouping (₹9,999 · ₹1,25,000). Never $ or US$ in a template, a hook or a caption.
- PLACEHOLDER starting prices (confirm or replace with the owner's real rates):
  - Landing page (1 page): ₹9,999, live in about 7 days
  - Business website (up to 5 pages): ₹19,999, about 10-14 days
  - Store or booking site: ₹34,999, about 14-21 days
  Two rounds of changes included. Prices are "from" prices; say so.
- A price shown in a template is the studio's real offer (even though the brand in the template is fictional), so use the numbers above and nothing bigger.

## What may be claimed
- Allowed: what is delivered, how fast, for how much, what is included, how the process works, clear before/after of a design.
- Not allowed: invented results or statistics ("+212% leads", "3.1x return", "₹2.4 lakh a month recovered", uptime percentages, client counts, testimonials with
  numbers). The studio has no client results yet; fake numbers cost trust and may breach advertising rules for misleading claims.
- If a result is shown to demonstrate a layout, it must be obviously an example label ("example") and never the hook.

## Concepts that fit a beginner studio in India
Cafés and restaurants, clinics and dentists, coaching and tuition centres, salons and boutiques, gyms, D2C and Instagram sellers, wedding and event services, real
estate agents, photographers, small agencies. Local touches that signal "made for you": WhatsApp button, UPI / payment link, GST invoice, Google Maps pin, Hindi/English
mix in short labels, festival and season moments. Keep every name fictional and the copy original.

## Concepts to avoid until the owner says the studio really offers them
Analytics consultancy, API or developer-platform work, AI automation, enterprise SaaS, launch-film production, brand-strategy retainers.

## Hooks for this market
Questions an Indian small-business owner actually asks: "Website banwane ka kharcha kitna hota hai?" style curiosity in plain English works too: "What does a website cost in
₹?", "Can my café get orders without Swiggy?", "Is your Instagram page enough?", "How fast can my website go live?". Keep to 8 words, answer inside 18 seconds,
and let the answer be a real number or a real step the studio can stand behind.

## Open questions for the owner (the advisor asks, you answer once)
1. Your real starting prices and delivery times per service.
2. Which services you can really deliver today, and which you cannot yet.
3. A contact method for the link-in-bio page (WhatsApp number, email, or booking link).
