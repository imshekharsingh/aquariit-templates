Owner, 22 Nov: "pick up technological aspects that are discussed in the market today and design such an animation around it that people
get hooked to knowing better." Research: design/tech-research-2026-11-22.md. Prototype: techkit/scenes/proto-ram-squeeze.html (first 6 s).
Status: owner approved (22 Nov 2026, owner chat: 'I like this though ... go with your plan'): the owner's own words in the Owner chat at ~21:10-21:14 IST
("push it, and fix those three things first ... (I like this though)" on the 6 s prototype, then "installed both, go with your plan" on the plan: the full first

This page sets the look; design/rules-tech.md keeps the facts, brand and safety rules (F2-F4, V1-V4) and drops what is listed at the end.

## 1. Topic: this week's tech, explained by one picture
- Every topic is in the news this week or month (launch, chip, AI feature, price shift, India launch) and cited with a date; evergreen
  trivia ("why the light is green") only when a launch makes it news.
- The test: can a stranger say why it matters to *their* next purchase in one sentence? If not, pick another.

## 2. The look: one bold, elegant visual system
- **Ground**: near-black ink `#07080c` with one soft key light (a single radial glow behind the hero). No grids, no blobs, no background noise.
- **Colour**: white and greys for form, plus **at most two accents**: *signal* (electric cyan `#38e1ff`, what the topic is about) and
  *heat* (amber `#ffae3d`, the force acting on it). Nothing else is coloured.
- **Hero**: one big, recognisable object drawn well (a phone, a chip, a lens), 60-90% of the frame width, with real materials: a metal rim
  with a highlight, glass with one reflection, a chip with its gold contacts and laser marking. Detail lives on the object, not around it.
- **Type**: Inter Display Black, one bold word or number on screen at a time (120-260 px), set in the empty part of the frame, never over
  the object. Captions are the only other text (stable, bottom third). No header, no title on every frame, no chips, no pills, no labels
  under 40 px.
- **Depth without clutter**: foreground motion (particles, flying parts) is out of focus (blurred, low alpha), the hero is sharp.

## 3. Camera language (4+ distinct shots per reel)
- Shots: **macro** (a part fills the frame), **hero** (the whole object, 3/4 or front), **wide / scale** (the object tiny against what acts
  on it), **inside** (through the shell), **side or top** (a new angle on the same object). A reel uses at least four, each change 3x in scale
  or a new angle.
- Moves between shots: a whip (fast eased pan with motion blur), a push-through (zoom into a part until it becomes the next shot), a pull-out
  reveal. A new shot every 2-4 s; never more than 4 s on one framing.
- Every cut lands on a sound hit; FLOW's declared-cut list carries them.

## 4. Story shape (20-30 s)
1. **0 s, the hook picture + claim**: motion already happening, the claim as one big word or number ("8 GB -> 4 GB").
2. **2-6 s, the turn**: the camera follows the action to its cause (the shot change).
3. **The 'aha' visual**: one picture that makes the mechanism obvious muted (memory feeding the AI server).
4. **The takeaway**: what to do with it, on the object ("Buy the RAM you need now").
5. **The loop**: the last shot returns to the first framing mid-motion; the comment question in the caption.

## 5. What to DROP from the current techkit rules (they push toward flat, cluttered reels)
- The shared frame as default (techkit/base.html header y 250, chapter chip y 408, panel y 1240, recap pills): it repeats the title on every
  frame and squeezes the hero into a band. New reels build their own full frame.
- D6 "10+ micro-details" and D2 "6 named parts with labels": they produce tiny chips and labels; replace with "detail on the object's
  material, labels only as the one bold word".
- D3 "three depth planes in every chapter" and D4 "2 secondary loops in every chapter": they invite noise; keep one foreground layer, and
  motion that tells the story.
- Output QA HERO's "55% change inside the subject box" as a target: it rewards wobbling a drawing rather than changing the shot; keep it as
  a warning only for this system, the critic judges clarity and beauty.
- The X format colour worlds (four palettes): replaced by one system (ink, white, signal, heat) so the channel is recognisable in a grid.
- Fixed line counts (7 lines, 100-140 words): length follows the story (20-30 s).

## 6. The craft check before any render (the critic's questions)
1. At phone size, can you name the object in each still in under a second?
2. Is there exactly one bold word or number per still, and is it outside the object?
3. Do four consecutive stills show four different framings?
4. Muted, does the 0-3 s strip make you want to know why?
5. Is anything on screen that is not white, grey, signal or heat?
