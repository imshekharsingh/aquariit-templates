# A new episode in a learned style

A good episode is one the series' own viewers would recognise as the same KIND of video: its structure, pacing, graphics grammar and sound
grammar, on a new topic, in our voice, our look and our copy. Never use a creator's face, voice, footage, music, logo, show name, characters
or art, and never a near-identical look of one specific piece (CLAUDE.md, design/safety.md). Their names stay out of our reels and captions.

## Study the series

1. Take 2-3 examples of the series (from the owner's Inbox or references). Study each with `/reverse-any-video` and write one pattern card each
   in design/references.md.
2. Write a short **style card** across them: what repeats in every episode (the hook shape, chapter rhythm, caption skin, title treatment,
   transitions, sound hits), with the numbers: cuts a minute, pause density, longest hold, loudness, sound effects on picture changes,
   words a minute.
3. Note which topics the style is good for and which it is not. A topic it does not fit: say so and pick the nearest angle that fits.

## Make the episode in our kit

- Map each pattern to our kit and our channel's rulebook (design/rules-*.md); where the style breaks one of our rules, our rule wins.
- Before any graphic, find the series' precedent for that kind of moment on the style card; with none, do not add it.
- Facts first, each with its source (CLAUDE.md); script to our voice rules; then the usual flow: stills, Premium check, preflight, ONE render.
- Report our numbers beside the style card's (cuts a minute, longest hold, loudness, sound hits) in the reel's review file, and name every check
  that failed.
- Keep a short GAPS line in the review file: where the style card was missing, unclear or wrong. It is how the card gets better.
